Good to know

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is from 12:00 PM and check-out is by 10:00 AM. Early check-in or late check-out can be arranged if the room is free. Ask when you book.

Are pets allowed?

Yes, pets are welcome. Tell us when you book so we can set you up with the right room.

How many guests can the lawn hold?

Up to 350 guests seated or 550 standing, with parking for 300 cars and 350 bikes.

Can we bring our own caterer or decorator?

Outside caterers and decorators are usually allowed with approval, and in-house catering is available. Confirm details with the team when you enquire.

How does the advance payment work?

Pay a small advance online with UPI, card or netbanking to hold your dates. The balance is paid at the resort. Cancellation terms are shared on your booking confirmation.

Is the resort suitable for families with children?

Yes. There's open lawn space to play and a pool. Children should be supervised near the water.

Tell us about your plans

Weddings, a family weekend or a team outing. We reply on WhatsApp or phone, usually within a few hours.
